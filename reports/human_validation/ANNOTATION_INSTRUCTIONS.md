# Moodify human song-emotion validation

This is an in-project validation workflow, not a Google Form.

## Task
For each song, listen to enough of the track to judge its **overall dominant emotional expression**. Select exactly one of:

- `sadness` — sorrow, loneliness, grief, heartbreak, loss, longing
- `joy` — happiness, celebration, fun, excitement, optimism, positive energy
- `love` — romantic affection, devotion, attachment, being in love
- `anger` — rage, hostility, frustration, resentment, confrontation, revenge
- `fear` — fear, anxiety, danger, threat, dread, horror, insecurity
- `surprise` — shock, astonishment, unexpected change, sudden realization, wonder
- `unclear` — none fits well or there is not enough information

Judge the **song**, not the artist, popularity, cover art, or your personal preference. Do not look at Moodify's predicted label while annotating.

## Confidence
- `high`: clear dominant emotion
- `medium`: reasonable but some ambiguity
- `low`: difficult to decide

## Intensity
1 = very weak, 5 = very strong.

## Recommended study design
Use 3 independent annotators per song for the 300-song pilot. The queue is balanced across English, Hindi, and Punjabi catalogue entries and across popularity ranges. It is a validation sample, not a replacement for the entire catalogue.
